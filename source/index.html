WATER TEST

Use one fresh strip

  1. Dip the strip into the spa and remove it immediately.
  2. Do not shake off the excess water.
  3. Hold the strip level.
  4. For automatic scanning, photograph the strip roughly straight with the reagent tip toward the top or left of the photo.
  5. Use the 15-second timer if you want help with timing, or skip it if the strip is already ready to read.
15
Ready when you are. The timer is optional. If the strip is already at its read time, take or choose a photo now.
